**How I accessed sources.** The session's network policy blocked arxiv.org, huggingface.co, projectaria.com, thecvf.com, ecva.net, zenodo.org and PMC for direct fetching. **Takeaway.** No dataset found meets all five criteria. In particular, I found **no real crowd dataset with a released raw camera IMU and independent 3D GT for the people around**. The crowd datasets (InCrowd-VI, SiT, JRDB, CrowdBot) either lack people GT, or have robot-frame boxes and unclear IMU or ego-pose GT. The datasets with accurate other-person GT (ADT, Nymeria, EgoHumans, AEA) have only 1–4 people in view.

## 2. Ranked shortlist (criteria 1–3)

### 1. Aria Digital Twin (ADT)
**Why it ranks first:**
- Camera GT comes from mocap, not SLAM ([paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2306.06362v2)).
- The other person's full skeleton is in the *same* Scene frame ([skeleton provider](https://github.com/facebookresearch/projectaria_tools/blob/main/projects/AriaDigitalTwinDatasetTools/data_provider/AriaDigitalTwinSkeletonProvider.h)).
- The raw VRS carries both IMUs ([ADT page](https://www.projectaria.com/datasets/adt/)).
- Multi-person sequences are flagged: `kIsMultiPersonKey` and `kConcurrentSequenceNameKey` are in the metadata ([source](https://github.com/facebookresearch/projectaria_tools/blob/main/projects/AriaDigitalTwinDatasetTools/data_provider/AriaDigitalTwinDataPathsProvider.cpp)).

**Weaknesses:** indoor, at most about 2 people, no crowd.

**Conversion work:**
- **Camera model.** Aria cameras are fisheye; projectaria_tools documents its intrinsic models ([Camera Intrinsic Models](https://facebookresearch.github.io/projectaria_tools/docs/tech_insights/camera_intrinsic_models)). The model name "FisheyeRadTanThinPrism / Fisheye624" is **unverified** in the sources I could read. SLAM cameras are 640×480 global shutter; RGB is 1408×1408 rolling shutter (2880×2880 max) ([hardware spec](https://facebookresearch.github.io/projectaria_tools/docs/tech_spec/hardware_spec)).
- **Rectification.** Either keep the fisheye model (if the VIO under test supports it) or undistort to a pinhole with projectaria_tools calibration utilities. The exact function name is **unverified**.
- **Time sync.** The skeleton is queried by device time in ns (`getSkeletonByTimestampNs`, `TimeDomain::DeviceTime`) ([source](https://github.com/facebookresearch/projectaria_tools/blob/main/projects/AriaDigitalTwinDatasetTools/data_provider/AriaDigitalTwinSkeletonProvider.h)). Images and IMU in the VRS share the same device clock. So no cross-device sync is needed within one recording. The concurrent second-wearer recording is already in the same Scene frame.
- **Frame alignment.** None is needed for the people GT (Scene frame). Only the device-to-camera extrinsics from the calibration are needed, to turn the device pose into a camera pose.

### 2. Nymeria (use the observer recording as the "robot")
**Why it ranks second:**
- The observer Aria provides raw VRS (`recording_observer/data/data.vrs`, `motion.vrs`) and MPS SLAM trajectories ([definitions.py](https://github.com/senekit/Nymeria_dataset/blob/main/nymeria/definitions.py)).
- The participant's XSens body is registered into the same metric world as all Aria devices ([projectaria.com](https://www.projectaria.com/datasets/nymeria/)).
- It is huge and covers indoor and outdoor scenes ([paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.10984)).

**Weaknesses:**
- Camera GT is MPS SLAM, not mocap.
- Usually one GT person.
- Body motion is "optimized for spatial alignment with the glasses" of the participant, not the observer ([NymeriaPlus](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.18496v1)). Expect some residual error when projecting into the observer's view.

**Conversion work:**
- Same Aria camera model and rectification as ADT.
- **Time sync.** Hardware timecode across all devices, drift under 4.2 ms per session ([search extract of the paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.09905v1)). The observer-to-participant timestamp mapping must use the timecode domain. The exact API is **unverified**.
- **Frame alignment.** Already in a shared world. Validate by projecting the body head joint into the observer's RGB and SLAM images.

### 3. Aria Everyday Activities (AEA)
**Why it ranks third:** it meets criterion 3 only at the minimum level. The "other person" is the second wearer's Aria head trajectory, which shares the frame and is timecode-synchronised ([paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2402.13349)). Raw VRS with IMU is available.

**Conversion work:**
- Same as Nymeria.
- The head-to-ground offset must be approximated to obtain a root position. This is an assumption, not GT.

**Runner-up:** EgoHTR. It has the same observer setup as Nymeria, with mocap-validated SMPL-X, but its release contents are still **unverified** ([paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.13472)).

**Excluded from the shortlist:** EgoHumans is the best multi-person benchmark, but it fails criterion 1: no IMU is in the released layout ([DOWNLOAD.md](https://github.com/rawalkhirodkar/egohumans/blob/main/assets/DOWNLOAD.md)).

## 3. Top candidate (ADT): files and ROS2 conversion plan

### Files per sequence
File names come from [AriaDigitalTwinDataFileNames.h](https://github.com/facebookresearch/projectaria_tools/blob/main/projects/AriaDigitalTwinDatasetTools/data_provider/AriaDigitalTwinDataFileNames.h).

**Required:**
- `video.vrs`: RGB, two SLAM cameras, IMU-right (1 kHz) and IMU-left (800 Hz) ([VRS format](https://facebookresearch.github.io/projectaria_tools/docs/data_formats/aria_vrs/aria_vrs_format)), plus device calibration.
- `aria_trajectory.csv`: 6-DoF device GT in the Scene frame.
- `<SkeletonName>.json` and `skeleton_aria_association.json`: skeleton GT, and which skeleton belongs to which Aria device.
- `metadata.json`: scene, multi-person flag, concurrent sequence name.

**Useful:**
- `2d_bounding_box_with_skeleton.csv`: 2D person boxes, to evaluate the detector.
- `instances.json`
- `mps/`: MPS SLAM output, a baseline against mocap GT.

**Skip:** `depth_images*.vrs`, `segmentations*.vrs` and `synthetic_video.vrs`. These are large: the raw depth zip alone was 7.8 GB of a 12.4 GB episode ([Voxel51](https://voxel51.com/blog/aria-digital-twin-mcap-fiftyone)).

**Which sequences:** only those whose `metadata.json` marks them multi-person. Download **both** concurrent recordings, so each wearer can serve as the camera while the other is the target.

**Access:** accept the licence in Aria Dataset Explorer to obtain the download URLs ([Voxel51](https://voxel51.com/blog/aria-digital-twin-mcap-fiftyone)). The exact CLI name and data-group flags of the ADT downloader are **unverified**.

### Conversion to ROS2 bag (rough plan)
No ROS2 Aria converter was found. `vrs2ros` is ROS1 only and converts the SLAM cameras, RGB and IMU ([repo](https://github.com/nmerrill67/vrs2ros), MIT). A rosbag1 can be turned into a rosbag2 with the `rosbags` library ([discussion](https://discourse.openrobotics.org/t/ros1-bag-file-to-ros2-bag-file-converter/19995)). Writing directly with Python is simpler:

1. **Read** with `projectaria_tools`:
   - Iterate over the VRS streams `1201-1`/`1201-2` (SLAM left/right), the RGB stream, and `1202-1`/`1202-2` (IMU) ([VRS format](https://facebookresearch.github.io/projectaria_tools/docs/data_formats/aria_vrs/aria_vrs_format)), using device-time ns as the bag stamp.
   - Load the ADT providers for the trajectory and skeleton ([projectaria_tools](https://github.com/facebookresearch/projectaria_tools)).
2. **Images.**
   - Publish `sensor_msgs/Image` (mono8 for SLAM; rgb8 for RGB, downscaled if needed) on `/cam0/image_raw`, `/cam1/image_raw` and `/rgb/image_raw`.
   - Publish `sensor_msgs/CameraInfo` from the device calibration.
   - If the estimator needs a pinhole model, undistort first and publish pinhole `CameraInfo`. Otherwise export the fisheye parameters to a YAML file in the estimator's format, for example Kalibr ds/eucm. A refit may be needed; feasibility is **unverified**.
3. **IMU.**
   - Publish `sensor_msgs/Imu` on `/imu0` from one IMU (imu-right, 1 kHz).
   - Use the calibration's device-to-IMU and camera-to-IMU extrinsics to write `T_cam_imu` for the estimator.
   - Apply the IMU's factory intrinsics (bias, scale) if they are not already applied to the raw stream. Whether they are applied is **unverified**.
4. **GT transforms** (`tf2_msgs/TFMessage` on `/tf`, or a dedicated `/gt/...` topic):
   - `scene → device` from `aria_trajectory.csv`.
   - `device → cam0`, `device → imu0` as static transforms on `/tf_static`, from the calibration.
   - `scene → person_<id>/root` and one frame per joint from the skeleton provider, at the skeleton rate. Use the skeleton not associated with the recording device in `skeleton_aria_association.json`. Optionally also publish `visualization_msgs/MarkerArray`.
5. **Write** with `rosbag2_py` (or `rosbags`) as mcap or sqlite3.
6. **Validate.**
   - Project the GT person joints through `T_scene_cam` and `CameraInfo` onto the images.
   - Check the projections against `2d_bounding_box_with_skeleton.csv` to confirm frames and timing before using the bag.
